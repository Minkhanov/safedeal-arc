Escrow for freelance work, in USDC on Arc

The client locks the budget in a smart contract. The freelancer is paid milestone by milestone — when the client approves, or automatically once the review period ends without a dispute. No platform holds the money and nobody takes a fee.

  • Each side can always give money to the other. Taking money needs the other side's consent, an expired timer, or the arbiter.
  • Disputes go to an arbiter you both agreed on — or you settle on a split. Unresolved for 30 days: split 50/50.
  • One signature + one transaction funds the deal. Gas is paid in USDC, about a cent.
